Task Planner

    Plan Your Tasks

    Add tasks with deadlines and priorities. Mark them complete when finished or delete them if no longer needed.

      How to Use the Planner

      Enter your task name, select a due date, and choose a priority level. Once added, tasks will appear in your list below. You can:

      • ✅ Mark tasks as complete when finished
      • ❌ Delete tasks you no longer need
      • 📅 Sort tasks by date or priority (coming soon)

      This planner helps you stay organised and avoid last-minute stress.

      Planner Best Practices

      ✔ Break large assignments into smaller tasks
      ✔ Always set realistic deadlines
      ✔ Use High priority for urgent work, Medium for regular tasks, and Low for optional activities
