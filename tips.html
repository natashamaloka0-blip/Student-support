Study Tips & Time Management

Work for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break. Repeat this cycle to stay focused.

Test yourself by recalling information instead of just rereading notes.

Divide your day into blocks of time dedicated to specific tasks.

Do the most important tasks first to avoid last-minute stress.

Why These Tips Work

Each technique is backed by research in cognitive psychology and productivity studies:

  • Pomodoro: Prevents burnout by balancing focus and rest.
  • Active Recall: Strengthens memory by forcing your brain to retrieve information.
  • Time Blocking: Creates structure and reduces procrastination.
  • Healthy Habits: Sleep, exercise, and nutrition improve concentration.

Additional Advice

✔ Study in a quiet environment
✔ Remove distractions (phone, social media)
✔ Review notes daily instead of cramming
✔ Reward yourself after completing tasks
